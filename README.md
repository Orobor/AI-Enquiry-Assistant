# AI Enquiry Assistant – Academic Offences

## Overview

The **AI Enquiry Assistant** is a demonstration project designed to showcase the potential of AI-powered chatbots in answering student and staff enquiries based on university policy documents.

The application focuses on academic offences, enabling users to ask questions in natural language and receive AI-generated responses grounded in relevant university policies.

Example enquiries include:
- What are the consequences of an academic offence?
- Does an academic offence appear on my transcript?
- Who can access information about my academic offence?
- Can a former student be investigated?

## Technologies Used
- **React** – Frontend development framework.
- **Python & Django** – Backend development and application framework.
- **PostgreSQL** – Database management.
- **LangGraph** – AI workflow orchestration and conversation management.
- **Qwen2.5-7B** – Large Language Model (LLM) powering the assistant.

## Key Features

- Natural language question answering.
- Policy-grounded AI-generated responses.
- Conversational interaction for student and staff enquiries.
- Demonstration of how university policy documents can support AI-powered information services.

## Project Purpose

This project demonstrates how Large Language Models (LLMs), combined with workflow orchestration and university policy documents, can improve access to institutional information.

It explores the potential of AI-powered assistants to make university policies easier to navigate, helping students and staff find relevant information quickly and conveniently.

## Disclaimer

This is a demonstration project and is not an official university service. AI-generated responses are intended for informational purposes only and should not be treated as formal institutional decisions or advice. Users should refer to official university policies and appropriate university departments for authoritative guidance.
